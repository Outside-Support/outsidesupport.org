Online Peer Support Program · For Middle Schoolers

Your child doesn't have to face bullying alone.

Outside Support provides a free, six-week online support group for middle schoolers who have experienced bullying. Students are grouped with kids from other schools — never anyone from their own — giving them a safe space to learn problem-solving and coping skills while connecting with peers who get it.

Bullying leaves a mark that doesn't disappear when school ends.

Kids who've been bullied are more likely to struggle at school, have more difficulty with peer relationships, and find it tougher to cope with stress, all while feeling less supported.1 Prevention helps, but students who've already been targeted often need something built just for them. That's what BELONG offers.

Built on real research

BELONG was developed and piloted by Dr. Susan Swearer — Willa Cather Professor of Educational Psychology at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, a licensed psychologist, and director of the BRAVE Lab, one of the country's leading bullying research programs.

How we keep your child safe

  • Password-protected, HIPAA-compliant Zoom sessions with a waiting room — only enrolled students and the facilitator can join.
  • Facilitators are trained under Dr. Swearer and have emergency mental-health training.
  • Both parental consent and your child's assent are required before your child takes part.
BELONG is a peer-support and skill-building group — not therapy or clinical treatment. It's designed to help kids feel understood and build everyday skills alongside peers who get it.

For parents

Frequently asked questions.

What is the BELONG Program?
A free, six-week online support group for middle schoolers who have experienced bullying. Groups are led by facilitators and meet weekly in a small-group format to connect with peers who understand and build real skills and confidence to move forward. BELONG was developed and piloted with the BRAVE Lab at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln.
What's the connection between Outside Support and the University of Nebraska?
A partnership to bring BELONG to the kids who need it. BELONG was designed and piloted by Dr. Susan Swearer at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln's BRAVE Lab, with funding from our founder, Jim Moran. After seeing the difference it made, we created Outside Support — a nonprofit — to scale it to families across the country.
Is this therapy?
No — BELONG is a peer-support and skill-building group, not therapy or clinical treatment. It helps kids feel understood and build practical, everyday skills. (For mental-health or crisis support, resources can be found here.)
Who leads the groups?
Groups are led weekly by the same facilitators, trained under Dr. Susan Swearer — a licensed psychologist and director of the BRAVE Lab. Current groups are run by doctoral students at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln.
How do you keep my child safe?
Sessions are held on password-protected, HIPAA-compliant Zoom links with a waiting room, so only enrolled students and the facilitator can join — no one from your child's own school, ever. Facilitators are trained by Dr. Swearer and have emergency mental-health training. Both parental consent and your child's assent are required before your child takes part.
Is it really free?
Completely free for families — no fees, no contracts, ever.
When and where are groups held?
Groups are fully online and outside of school hours. They meet for 60 minutes once a week over the six weeks, on a private Zoom link. We work with you to place your child in a group that fits their schedule — all you need is a device with a camera and an internet connection.
What if my child misses a session?
BELONG works because the same small group meets each week — that consistency is what builds trust. For that reason there are no makeup sessions, so we ask that you only sign up for a time your child can attend all six. We know life happens, but the more consistently each child attends, the more everyone gets out of the group.
Will my child be with kids from their school?
Never. Students come from across the country, so your child is never grouped with anyone from their own school — that's our guarantee.
What if my child needs more help than a group can offer?
BELONG isn't a crisis or mental-health service. If your child is in immediate danger, call or text 988 or call 911. For ongoing mental-health support, additional resources can be found here.

Are you a school counselor?

BELONG is a no-cost Tier 2 resource you can refer students to with confidence — no classroom time, no added workload. We handle the family outreach from there.
