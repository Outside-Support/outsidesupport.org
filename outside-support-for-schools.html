Where We Fit

A Tier 2 support that slots right into your MTSS framework.

Your school's Tier 1 efforts — assemblies, classroom curricula, school-wide culture initiatives — do important work. But some students need more targeted support than universal programming can provide.

The BELONG Program is designed for exactly those students. It's a structured, small-group Tier 2 intervention for middle schoolers who've experienced bullying — research-informed, facilitator-led, and focused on the student who was targeted.

Because it runs online and outside school hours, it requires no classroom time, no staff coverage, and no scheduling disruption. You refer the student. We do the rest.

What We Offer

Built to work alongside what you already do.

Outside Support is a partner, not a replacement. Here's what referring a student actually looks like from your end.

Research-informed curriculum

The BELONG Program is grounded in research from the BRAVE Lab at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln — one of the country's leading bullying research programs. When you refer a student, you're referring them to something that works.

Fully online, no disruption

Sessions happen online, outside school hours. No classroom time lost, no scheduling conflicts, no staff required on your end. Students attend from home, on a schedule that works for their family.

No one from their school

Because the program draws students from across the country, we guarantee that no student is ever grouped with someone from their own school. That's what makes middle schoolers actually willing to open up.

Grouped by gender

Students are placed in small groups of peers the same gender. That intentional structure makes the program more relevant and the peer connections more meaningful.

Trained facilitators

Every group is led by a facilitator trained in the BELONG Program curriculum. Consistent, intentional, and quality-controlled — every session, every group.

We handle the family outreach

Once you make the introduction, we take it from there — connecting with families, explaining the program, and managing enrollment. Minimal lift on your end.

Our Research Foundation

Built on the BRAVE Lab's bullying research.

The BELONG Program is directly built on the work of the BRAVE Lab at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. The BELONG pilot, run through the lab, tested this approach with real middle schoolers: students left with a better understanding of their experiences, stronger problem-solving skills, and — critically — the sense that they weren't alone.

Dr. Susan Swearer — Willa Cather Professor of Educational Psychology at UNL, a licensed psychologist, and director of the BRAVE Lab — serves as a research advisor to Outside Support.

For Counselors

Frequently asked questions.

What is BELONG, and how does it fit with what we already do?
BELONG is a free support group for middle schoolers who’ve experienced bullying, developed from evidence-based research — small, facilitator-led groups that help students build real skills and connect with peers. It builds on the bullying-prevention work you’re already doing without adding to your plate: you refer a student, and we handle the rest — no classroom time, no staff coverage, no scheduling disruption.
What’s the connection between Outside Support and the University of Nebraska?
A partnership to bring the BELONG Program to the kids who need it. BELONG was designed and piloted by Dr. Susan Swearer at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln’s BRAVE Lab, with funding from our founder, Jim Moran. After seeing the difference it made for kids and parents, we created Outside Support — a nonprofit — to scale the program to families across the country.
Is it therapy or counseling?
No. BELONG is a peer-support and skill-building group — not therapy or clinical treatment, and not a replacement for school counseling or mental-health services. It’s an additional resource that complements the support you already provide.
Who is it for?
Middle schoolers in grades 6–8 who’ve experienced bullying and could benefit from peer connection and skill building.
How do I refer a student?
Submit a short referral form with your and a parent’s contact information. We follow up, connect with the family, explain the program, and manage consent and enrollment from there. Minimal lift on your end.
Is there any cost to my school?
None. BELONG is completely free to schools and families — no fees, no contracts, and no administrative burden.
Will students be grouped with others from our school?
Never. Because we draw students from across the country, we can guarantee a student is never placed in a group with someone from their own school.
Who leads the groups, and are they qualified?
Groups are led weekly by the same facilitators, trained under Dr. Susan Swearer — a licensed psychologist and director of the BRAVE Lab at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. Current groups are run by doctoral students there.
How do you keep students safe?
Password-protected, HIPAA-compliant Zoom sessions limited to enrolled students and the facilitator, led by trained facilitators. Both parental consent and youth assent are required before any student takes part.
How can I bring this program to my school?
We’d love to talk it through — email us at team@outsidesupport.org, and we’ll find what fits your school. A few easy ways to start:
  • Share BELONG with your middle school counseling team so they can refer students who could benefit.
  • Add Outside Support to your community resource list.
  • Include a short blurb and a link to outsidesupport.org in a counselor newsletter to parents.
