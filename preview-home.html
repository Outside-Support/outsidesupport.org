Bullying leaves a mark that doesn't disappear when school ends.

Kids who've been bullied are more likely to struggle at school, have more difficulty with peer relationships, and find it tougher to cope with stress, all while feeling less supported.1 Prevention helps, but students who've already been targeted often need something built just for them. That's what BELONG offers.

1 Drawn from peer-reviewed research on the effects of bullying. For references.

The BELONG Program

Six weeks to build skills and find connection.

Developed and piloted with the BRAVE Lab at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. Small online groups, led by trained facilitators, built around three core aims.

01

Build understanding

Kids learn what bullying is, why it happens, and — critically — that it's never their fault. Naming the experience is the first step to moving past it.

02

Develop skills

Practical, age-appropriate skills — problem-solving, coping strategies for stressful moments, and how to assertively ask for help.

03

Create connection

Small facilitator-led groups where real, empathetic peer connections form — and because it's online, never with anyone from their own school.

Grounded in research · Proven with kids

Grounded in research, built for kids.

BELONG is built directly on the work of the BRAVE Lab at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln — one of the country's leading bullying research programs — and was developed by Dr. Susan Swearer, a licensed psychologist and the lab's director. In the pilot, kids left with a better understanding of their experience, stronger problem-solving skills, and the sense that they weren't alone.
