What the program covers

What your child will work on.

Not a lecture or a workshop — the same small group of peers, led by the same trained facilitator, every week. That consistency is what makes the connection real.

01

Understanding bullying

A clear, grounded understanding of what bullying is — and that it's never their fault. That shared language helps them make sense of their experience and find the words to talk about it.

02

Problem solving

Real strategies for navigating bullying — how to respond in the moment, and how to handle conflict inside their own friend group, which is often the hardest kind.

03

Building resilience

Kids learn the link between how they think, feel, and act — and build real confidence to move forward.

“He always came out of those sessions feeling very positive, and good about things. Being able to relate to his particular thing was always the most helpful for him.”

Parent of a participant · BELONG pilot, University of Nebraska
Why online works

Built for kids and their families.

Grouped by gender so the conversation feels relevant and the connections feel natural.

Flexible online scheduling — no commute, no disruption. We find a time that fits your family.

A trained facilitator guides every session, so your child is in consistent, capable hands.

Always free. Cost should never be why a child doesn't get support.

No one from their school. Ever.
Because we draw students from across the country, your child is never grouped with someone from their own school — that's what makes kids willing to open up.
Why you can trust us

Serious research. Careful hands.

Built on real research

BELONG was developed and piloted by Dr. Susan Swearer — Willa Cather Professor of Educational Psychology at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, a licensed psychologist, and director of the BRAVE Lab, one of the country's leading bullying research programs.

In partnership with the BRAVE Lab · UNL

How we keep your child safe

  • Facilitators are trained under Dr. Swearer and have emergency mental-health training.
  • Parental consent is required before any child takes part.
  • Never grouped with anyone from their own school, anywhere in the country.
BELONG is a peer-support and skill-building group — not therapy or clinical treatment. It's designed to help kids feel understood and build everyday coping skills alongside peers who get it.
